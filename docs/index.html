03 / TECHNICAL CHECK

What's missing technically

Visibility isn't just about content. A technical check of the sample clinic's website found three basic gaps — and these are the first things AI looks at when it reads and cites a website.

MISSING

llms.txt

There is no llms.txt file in the site's root directory. This file is a site guide written specifically for AI models to read — without it, AI has to guess what your clinic does.

MISSING

FAQ content

The website has no FAQ section. Most questions patients ask AI are phrased as questions and answers — if your site has no matching Q&A content, AI has no answer of yours to cite.

INCOMPLETE

Schema / JSON-LD

The structured data covers only Organization. With no more detailed clinic, service, and location information, AI has a hard time confirming your clinic type, service area, and where you're located.

07 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is a GEO audit different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO looks at where your website ranks in Google search results. A GEO audit looks at what happens when a patient asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Google AI Mode directly — whether your clinic appears in AI's answer at all. Named, cited, and recommended are each counted separately, run by run.

Is the 0/40 in the sample a full-audit result?

No. The sample is the first-batch snapshot from 2026-11-19: 5 patient questions × 3 engines × 3 runs each — 45 runs planned, 40 completed. A full audit is 15 questions × 4 engines × 3 runs each, for 180 runs total.

After the audit, can you guarantee AI will recommend my clinic?

No. No ranking, citation, mention, traffic, or revenue growth is guaranteed. The report is a snapshot of the day it was measured, and AI models change their answers every day. What the audit does deliver is a measurement of where you stand, who you're losing to, your technical gaps, and a prioritized Top 10 fix list with a 30-day plan.

Will you make the fixes on the list for us?

No. Implementation is not included in the audit — we don't modify your website or write content for you. The fix list and 30-day plan spell out what to change and in what order, so your clinic can do it in-house or hand it to your current website provider.

Why ask each question 3 times?

Because AI answers aren't fixed: ask the same question several times and the clinics named can change. Running each question 3 times and counting named / cited / recommended across all runs is closer to what patients will actually see than asking just once.

How much does it cost?
